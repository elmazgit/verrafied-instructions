Workflow

How the mechanic uses Verrafied AI

Six simple steps from install to customer share-out. Screenshots show the current app experience.

Step 1

Download & Register

Create an account with shop or personal details, confirm registration, and sign in to your dashboard.

Step 2

Enter or Scan the VIN

Type the VIN or point the camera at the VIN plate. The app reads and attaches it to the inspection automatically.

Step 3

Record the vehicle video

Walk around the vehicle, explain the issue naturally, and upload the video through the app.

Step 4

AI converts speech to text

After upload, the AI converts spoken details into clean, organized text and builds a formatted inspection summary.

Step 5

Automatic professional report

The app bundles the original video with a written inspection report so customers get visual proof plus a clear explanation.

Step 6

Send to the customer

Share the inspection link in one tap directly from the app. (Link sharing only—no email or SMS sending.)
